These terms describe the conditions for using DockPDFConverter, an Android app for working with documents and images.

1. App features

DockPDFConverter lets you open and convert supported files, merge documents into a PDF, extract or rotate pages and share the results. The available operations depend on the format and characteristics of the file.

2. Use of documents

Only use the app with files you have the right to open, edit or share. Before sending a document, check the content of the result and the recipient, especially if the file contains personal data or confidential information.

3. Processing and sharing

DockPDFConverter processes documents on your device and leaves the original files untouched. When you choose to share a result, Android lets you select the app to pass it to. From that point on, the file is handled by the service you chose.

4. Results, signatures and original documents

A conversion may change the visual layout or not preserve all elements of the source file. Check the result before using it and keep the original when you need the signed document, the PEC certified email or the complete email message.

Extracting the content of a P7M file is not a verification of the digital signature. A modified or converted copy does not replace the signed original.

5. Working copies and results

The app may keep working copies and results on your device. If you want to keep a result, share it or save it to your preferred destination before deleting files from the app or clearing its data.

For support, use the contact shown in the "App support" section of the DockPDFConverter listing on Google Play. When reporting a problem, include your device model, the app version and the steps to reproduce it.
