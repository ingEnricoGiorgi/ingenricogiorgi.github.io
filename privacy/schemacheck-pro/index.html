Privacy Policy

SchemaCheck Pro

How SchemaCheck Pro handles page data, scan results, and preferences.

Last updated: November 12, 2026
Analysis runs in your browser. Scan results and preferences are stored locally. Venginoads does not receive your browsing data or reports.

About this policy

SchemaCheck Pro is a Chrome extension published by Venginoads. It checks JSON-LD structured data on e-commerce product pages and reports errors, warnings, and passed checks.

Information used for scans

The extension reads page URLs, titles, JSON-LD, visible product prices, and product links as needed for the scan. On accessible HTTP and HTTPS pages, it can run a local check when a page loads to update the toolbar badge. Opening the popup also scans the active page.

When you start a category scan, the extension requests linked product pages directly from the same website without sending browser credentials. The destination website receives those requests. The extension analyzes the responses in your browser.

Local storage and exports

The extension stores your language and theme preferences, up to 20 individual scan reports, and the latest completed category report in Chrome's local extension storage. Reports can include page URLs, titles, scan dates, and check results.

You can delete individual reports or clear the list in History. A new completed category scan replaces the previous category report. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage. CSV and JSON exports remain in the location where you saved them.

Data sharing and use

Venginoads does not receive your scan data, sell user data, or use it for advertising, credit assessment, or lending. The extension does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

SchemaCheck Pro's use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
