Privacy Policy

Mountain Goat Jumper

Information about how Mountain Goat Jumper handles your data and protects your privacy.

Last updated: August 21, 2026
Privacy first. Mountain Goat Jumper does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.

About this policy

Mountain Goat Jumper is developed and published by Venginoads. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Personal Data

Mountain Goat Jumper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The game does not require an account and does not collect information such as:

  • Name or email address
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Financial information
  • Advertising identifiers

Local Game Data

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on the user's device. This may include:

  • Game progress and unlocked levels
  • High scores and local Top 5 records
  • A locally entered leaderboard name
  • Selected and unlocked skins
  • Game settings and audio preferences

This information is not transmitted to Venginoads or to third parties. Local data can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game, subject to Android's storage and backup behavior.

Advertising and Analytics

Mountain Goat Jumper currently does not contain advertising and does not use advertising identifiers.

The current version does not use active analytics, cloud gameplay services, third-party crash reporting services, or online leaderboards.

Internet Access

The Android application may include the standard Internet permission, but the current gameplay does not use network connections to transmit user data.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Mountain Goat Jumper does not actively use third-party advertising, analytics, authentication, cloud storage, or payment SDKs.

Google Play may independently process technical information related to app distribution, downloads, crashes, or Android Vitals as part of the Google Play platform. Such processing is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Mountain Goat Jumper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Future Changes

Future versions of the game may introduce features such as in-app purchases, analytics, online services, or other functionality.

If this changes how user data is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features are released.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
